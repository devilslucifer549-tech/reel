HAND SCROLL v4

BUILD THE APK (no Android Studio needed)
  1. github.com > new repository > upload ALL files in this folder (keep the .github folder).
  2. Actions tab > "Build APK" > Run workflow (~5 min).
  3. Download artifact "HandScroll-apk", unzip, send app-debug.apk to your phone, tap to install.
  (Or open the folder in Android Studio and press Run.)

USE
  Open the app and keep pressing the big button: Allow camera > Turn on Accessibility > Download model > START.
  (Accessibility greyed out? Settings > Apps > Hand Scroll > ⋮ > Allow restricted settings.)
  Gestures: open palm UP = next reel, DOWN = previous, hold FIST 1s = play/pause, hold THUMBS-UP 1s = like.
  Extras: auto-open Instagram, last-gesture + daily counter, auto-stop timer, pause/stop buttons in notification,
  camera only on while Instagram is open and screen on.
